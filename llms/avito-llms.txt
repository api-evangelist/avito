# Avito

> Avito is Russia's largest online classifieds platform (avito.ru). Its public Developer Portal (developers.avito.ru) exposes 25 OpenAPI 3.0.0 REST APIs on api.avito.ru for business sellers and integrators, secured with OAuth 2.0 (authorization_code and client_credentials) and per-minute rate limiting.
